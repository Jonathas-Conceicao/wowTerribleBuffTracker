# Phase 57.4 Plan 01: metaReminder engine Summary

**A built-in class-buff reminder kind (`metaReminder`) that runs through the shared user-reminder paths in its own cast namespace. Its duration, rank coverage and known spell all come from one 14-row Forever table in Providers.lua, and Blood Pact reads the imp's rank from the pet spellbook.**
