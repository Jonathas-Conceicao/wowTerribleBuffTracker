as a racial tracker": racials are identified by key shape only (`metaSkill:<numeric id>`,

v7 is rewritten to drop the legacy slots instead of re-keying them, and `scripts/migrate-dryrun.js`
mirrors all of it with a new selftest fixture and stops reading `RACIAL_SPELLS` out of Providers.lua.

Purpose: a player whose data holds racial trackers must load cleanly with everything else intact, and
the migration chain must not depend on code that Plans 02-05 delete.
Output: BuffEngine.lua schema v12, catalogue-free v7/v8, Core.lua without the race-driven retry, an
updated dry-run spec with 13 passing selftest cases.
