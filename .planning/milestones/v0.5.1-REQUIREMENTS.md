## v0.5.1 Requirements

### Bug fixes (backlog 999.9, 999.17)

- [x] **EDM-08**: Clicking a TBT container in Edit Mode selects it without TBT calling any Blizzard
  Edit Mode method — `pcall(EditModeManagerFrame.ClearSelectedSystem, ...)` in `ns:SelectContainer`
  is gone. Blizzard's yellow highlight may linger alongside TBT's while both are selected; accepted.
- [x] **STEAL-09**: In Merge Mode, a charge spell whose buff is active shows the buff duration **and**
  its charge count at once, as the CDM's own icon does. Reproduced on retail with the Mage's
  Prismatic Barrier.

### Icons

- [x] **TAB-08**: The TBT Cooldowns, Buffs and Reminders tabs in the CDM settings window show
  `icon_cooldown`, `icon_buff` and `icon_reminder` from `Media/Textures`; the selected and hover
  states still draw. The TBT logo (`tbt_icon_64x64`) stays on the TOC `## IconTexture:` and the
  settings panel, unchanged.
- [x] **TAB-09**: The tracker dialog's General and Advanced side tabs show `icon_info` and
  `icon_advanced`; `INV_Misc_Book_09`, the `GM-icon-settings` atlas and its fallback icon are removed.
- [x] **INST-10**: `./scripts/install.bat` deploys `Media/Textures/*.blp` to every client folder it
  finds, and prunes deployed textures the repo no longer has.
- [x] **DIST-13**: The release zip ships `Media/Textures` and not `Media/Source`;
  `scripts/png2blp.js` rebuilds every BLP in `Media/Textures` from its PNG in `Media/Source`.
- [x] **TAB-10** *(added 2026-09-30 after the user's in-game review of 61-63)*: Every tab icon ships as
  a faction-themed pair, `icon_<name>_ally` and `icon_<name>_horde`; a Horde character sees the Horde
  set and every other character (a Neutral Pandaren included) the Alliance set. Resolved once per
  login — a character never changes faction mid-session.

### CLICK — Clickable reminders (backlog 999.18)

- [x] **CLICK-01**: Out of combat, left-clicking a shown reminder icon casts that reminder's cast
  spell — in **every** reminders container, the base Buff Reminders container and any the user adds.
- [x] **CLICK-02**: Clickable reminders are on by default and can be turned off; the option's tooltip
  states plainly that clicks work out of combat only.
- [x] **CLICK-03**: In combat, clicking a reminder does nothing, and entering or leaving combat with
  reminders shown raises no Lua error, blocked action or taint.
- [x] **CLICK-04**: A click area exists only where a reminder icon is shown: an empty reminders
  container, or one whose reminders are all unloaded or satisfied, has no clickable area, just as it
  draws no icon. The area matches its icon's on-screen position and size after login and after Edit
  Mode closes, and never intercepts a drag while Edit Mode is open.
- [x] **CLICK-05**: Hovering a clickable reminder still shows that reminder's tooltip.
- [x] **CLICK-06**: A reminder's Advanced settings carry a cast spell, defaulting to the reminder's
  own spell and editable by the user. Built-in reminders ship with it set; a built-in with no
  castable spell has **no click action** — Forever's Blood Pact (an imp ability) is the named case.
- [x] **REM-05** *(added 2026-09-30 after the user's in-game review of 61-63)*: A reminder starts
  showing before its buff runs out — for the last 10% of the buff's duration, never less than 1
  second — carrying the buff's actual remaining time (sweep and countdown; the aura's real duration
  whenever it was readable), and is clickable like any shown reminder. A timer with no duration, or
  an indefinite one, keeps its reminder hidden until the buff is gone.

### MREM — Retail class-buff reminder suggestions

- [x] **MREM-04**: On retail, the Reminders tab's Suggested section offers the class-buff reminders
  the character knows. Mage is specified: **Arcane Intellect** (casts 1459) and **Arcane Familiar**
  (loads when talent 205022 is known, checks aura 210126, casts 1459 — Arcane Intellect grants the
  familiar when the talent is known).
- [x] **MREM-05**: Retail rows for the other classes, from the buffs, IDs and rules the user supplies
  when the phase starts. **Supplied 2026-09-30** (table in `64-CONTEXT.md`): Priest Power Word:
  Fortitude, Druid Mark of the Wild and Symbiotic Relationship, Warrior Battle Shout, Shaman
  Skyfury and Lightning Shield (192106, added 2026-10-01), Evoker Blessing of the Bronze and Source of Magic, Paladin Devotion Aura (permanent,
  satisfied by Concentration or Crusader Aura). Rows are tagged per client so a retail ID that also
  exists on Forever is never offered there; Symbiotic Relationship and Source of Magic click-cast
  on your target instead of yourself.

## Out of Scope

- **Clicking in combat** — secure buttons cannot be changed in combat; out-of-combat only is the
  design, not a gap.
- **Right-click, modifier-click or targeted casts** — left click casts the cast spell **on the
  player** (`unit = "player"`, user decision 2026-09-30: a reminder tracks the player's own missing
  buff), at the highest rank known; nothing else. Exception (user decision 2026-09-30): the two
  ally-cast built-ins, Symbiotic Relationship and Source of Magic, cast on your target.
- **Moving the TBT logo** — it stays in the repo root, on the TOC and the settings panel.
- **999.5, 999.8, 999.10** — not in this milestone; 999.8 and 999.10 stay parked on Blizzard's CDM
  changes.
