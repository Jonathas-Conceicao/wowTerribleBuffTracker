# Requirements — v0.5.8 Headmaster's Charge reminder

**Defined:** 2026-11-26, at kickoff, with the user. Research done before kickoff, inline: every
Wowhead Forever BiS guide (all classes and specs) was swept for endgame gear whose Use grants a long
buff; the item and buff were read from Wowhead's `/forever/` tooltips. Headmaster's Charge is the only
one a player can keep up.

**Core value:** Players can see countdown timers for buffs/cooldowns that the game no longer surfaces
automatically.

**Scope rule (user, carried from v0.5.6):** nothing works differently from today except what these
requirements name. The row is data through `MetaReminderRow`; the only new behaviour is the
equipped-item offer and the equipped-slot click.

## v0.5.8 Requirements

### Headmaster's Charge (Forever only)

- [x] **HCHG-01**: On Forever, a Headmaster's Charge reminder exists, keyed on the buff
  [18264](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=18264) (+20 Intellect to party members within 30 yd),
  15 min. It hides while the aura is up, whoever's staff applied it. It is not registered on retail.
- [x] **HCHG-02**: Suggested offers the Headmaster's Charge reminder only while the staff
  [13937](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=13937) is equipped (an equipped-item check, not a
  known-spell or bag check). The offer follows the staff being equipped or taken off. Every other
  row's offer is unchanged.
- [x] **HCHG-03**: A placed Headmaster's Charge reminder loads on every Forever character, with or
  without the staff, and keeps showing while the buff is missing until the user removes it (user
  decision, like Well-Rested). Every other row's load rule is unchanged, and the user's Load setting
  (Never) still applies.
- [x] **HCHG-04**: A click on the reminder, out of combat, uses the main-hand slot (`/use 16`) only
  while Headmaster's Charge is equipped there. With the staff not equipped (in the bags or absent),
  the click does nothing, never equips anything and raises no Lua error. The overlay follows the
  staff being equipped or taken off. The game's own "item not ready" message on cooldown is
  acceptable.

## Out of Scope

- **Starting a timer on the click.** The reminder watches the aura's presence, like every reminder.
- **Mark of the Dragon Lord (13143)** — a 30 min self shield that drops when it breaks; not in any
  Forever BiS list.
- **Heart of the Scale (13164)** — a 5 min buff on a 30 min cooldown; a "missing" reminder would nag
  most of the time.
- **Short on-use trinkets** (Blackhand's Breadth, Weakness Analyzer and the like) — combat bursts,
  not reminders.
- **Using the staff from the bags** — a `/use` on an unequipped weapon would equip it.
