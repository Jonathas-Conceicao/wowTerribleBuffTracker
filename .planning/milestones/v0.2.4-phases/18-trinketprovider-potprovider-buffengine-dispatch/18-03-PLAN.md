  <how-to-verify>
    **Deploy before testing:**
    1. Run `./scripts/install.bat` to copy Providers.lua, BuffEngine.lua, Display.lua (and all other addon files) to the WoW retail addons folder.

    **In WoW:**
    2. `/reload` to load the updated addon. Confirm no Lua errors appear at load.

    **Step 1 — Load and UI sanity:**
    3. Type `/tbt` to open the CDM settings panel and click the TBT tab. Confirm Suggested section renders (trinket, pot, lust icons should appear if the addon has been used before; first-run users may see placeholders).
    4. Close CDM (`/tbt` again or Escape).

    **Step 2 — User-tracked spell regression check (Phase 17 regression guard):**
    5. Cast any user-tracked spell (one of your own buffs that the addon tracks). Confirm: bar appears immediately, correct icon, correct label, countdown starts, bar disappears when the spell expires.

    **Step 3 — Trinket provider dispatch (D-01, success criterion 1):**
    6. Equip a tracked on-use trinket from the list (any of: Light Company Guidon, Vaelgor's Final Stare, Emberwing Feather, Algeth'ar Puzzle Box, Echo of L'ura, Radiant Sunstone, Freightrunner's Flask, Seed of Radiant Hope, Void Execution Mandate). Activate it. Confirm: a timer bar labeled with the trinket's SPELL name (not the item name) appears with the spell icon, countdown equal to the entry duration in ns.TRINKET_SPELLS.

    **Step 4 — Trinket reproc overwrite (D-03, success criterion 3):**
    7. While the trinket timer is still running (ideally mid-countdown), trigger it again (swap trinkets and re-activate, OR if you have two tracked trinkets equipped, activate the second one). Confirm: the bar's label and icon update to the newly cast trinket and countdown restarts from full duration. The previous trinket's entry is GONE — only one trinket bar visible at a time.

    **Step 5 — Pot provider dispatch (D-01, success criterion 2):**
    8. Use a tracked damage potion (Light's Potential, Potion of Recklessness, Draught of Rampant Abandon, or Void-Shrouded Tincture). Confirm: timer bar with pot spell icon and label appears with correct duration.

    **Step 6 — Pot reproc overwrite (D-03, success criterion 3):**
    9. If you have multiple tracked pots and the cooldown allows, trigger a different pot while the first is still active. Confirm: bar updates to the new pot and countdown restarts. Only one pot bar visible at a time.

    **Step 7 — Cancellation scan (D-15, D-16):**
    10. Cast a user-tracked spell that can be dispelled, right-clicked off, or cancelled via /cancelaura. Dispel/cancel it. Confirm: the bar disappears within roughly one aura update tick (UNIT_AURA event).
    11. (Optional but ideal) For trinket or pot cancellation: there's no natural dispel path, but if you have access to a way to strip the buff, confirm the bar disappears. If not feasible, rely on time-based expiry check in step 5/8.

    **Step 8 — Lust regression guard (Phase 19 scope — MUST NOT regress):**
    12. Trigger a lust/heroism (group-cast, drums, or bloodlust/time warp in a party). Confirm: lust bar appears with 40s duration, correct icon, correct label.

    **Step 9 — Preview mode (PITFALL-4 — pre-existing bug expected):**
    13. Open `/tbt`, scroll to see the CDM preview bars. Confirm: preview bars appear for all non-hidden trackedBuffs entries. Trinket and pot may show 0-second bars — this is the KNOWN PRE-EXISTING BUG documented in Phase 17 SUMMARY; it persists through Phase 20/21. NOT a Phase 18 regression as long as the preview behavior matches v0.2.3 exactly.

    15. If you have BugSack or similar error display, confirm no errors accumulated during any step above.
