    racial's cooldown tile, then cast it. Expected: the cooldown sweep starts, **no buff tile
    appears**, and **no Lua error**. Turn Lua errors on (`/console scriptErrors 1`) before this gate —
    a silent failure here is the exact defect the no-duration guard exists to prevent.

    **G6 — the indefinite tiles (SC 6, D-1 / D-6). Races: night elf, dwarf.**
    - Night elf, out of combat: use Shadowmeld. The **buff** tile appears and shows **no number and

    - Now move. Within a moment the buff tile disappears. (This is the aura-loss path; it is driven

    - Use Shadowmeld again and then **enter combat**. The buff tile disappears **immediately, on the

    - Dwarf: turn Find Treasure on. An indefinite buff tile appears with no number, and **no
      cooldown tile appears at all**. Enter and leave combat — the tile survives. Turn Find Treasure
      off and the tile goes.

    - Out of combat, in the condition that grants the long version (near a ley line / an elemental),

    - It must never shorten. If you ever see the tile jump **down**, that is a FAIL — correcting
      downward is explicitly not what D-3 asks for.
