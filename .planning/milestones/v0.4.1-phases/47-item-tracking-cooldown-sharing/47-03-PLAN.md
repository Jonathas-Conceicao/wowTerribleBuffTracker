<objective>
Make the tile draw. Audit **every** `trackerType` site in the codebase rather than trusting the
known list, widen the four gates in `Display.lua` that an item tracker must pass, and add the
item-count render that `ApplyChargeCount` structurally cannot do.

Purpose: this is where the phase's failures are silent instead of loud. A missed gate produces a
tracker that exists in the database, survives a reload, and simply never appears — no error, no log
line, nothing to grep for at runtime. The audit exists because the known list of three gates is
**wrong**: this plan's own pattern survey found five, and two of them (`SlotDraws` and the bar-slot
exclusion) are named in no prior document.

Output: a tracked item renders as an icon with its own texture, a running sweep driven by the plain
`(start, duration)` numbers Plan 01 stamps, and a count from Plan 01's tracked-count store. No new
Cooldown-widget code: `ApplyUserCooldown` is already generic on `entry.key` and `entry.duration`
and is consumed completely unmodified.

Decision IDs are defined in `47-01-PLAN.md`'s objective table. This plan is bound by **D-06**
(`Core.lua`'s debug cast/item log is protected — this plan does not touch `Core.lua` at all) and
renders the state D-02, D-03 and D-04 maintain.
</objective>
