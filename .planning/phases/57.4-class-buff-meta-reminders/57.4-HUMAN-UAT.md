## Tests

Run on the Forever beta (item 21 on Midnight retail) with `/console scriptErrors 1`. The TOC did not
change in this phase, so a /reload picks the build up (no full client restart needed). There is no
schema step. The user tests **Mage, Paladin and Warlock only** (user note 2026-09-29). The
Priest/Druid/Warrior/Hunter rows are item 23, which is skipped. TBT's ID tooltip adds "Aura spell ID: N"
to a hovered buff on your buff bar. SavedVariables are account-wide. Items marked **logout** need a
REAL logout/login.

Every per-buff item (1-8) expects the following. On that class, the Reminders tab's Suggested
section shows the buff's tile after the + and settings squares, and the tile's tooltip gives the spell
name and the table duration. Dragging the tile into Buff Reminders creates a tile there (TBT tab) and
shows the reminder on screen while the buff is missing. Casting the buff on yourself (any rank you
know) hides it at once and starts its timer (no swipe or timer text is drawn for a reminder). Hovering the buff on
your buff bar shows "Aura spell ID:" equal to the listed ID, or to the ID of the lower rank you cast.
If it differs, note the number, because the table row is then wrong. Right-click cancelling the buff
out of combat shows the reminder at once.
