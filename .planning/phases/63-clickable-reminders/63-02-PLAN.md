Build the secure click overlay module, `ReminderClick.lua`, exactly to the settled 999.18 design in
63-CONTEXT "Secure overlay" (binding): one pooled `SecureActionButtonTemplate` Button per clickable
reminder icon, parented to and anchored on `UIParent` only, placed in screen coordinates, combat
visibility owned by `RegisterStateDriver`, every protected write combat-gated and deferred to

shrunk -- frames cannot be destroyed); no known-spell check on the cast spell (the reminder's own load
rule already decides whether it is drawn, and a user-chosen cast spell the character does not know
simply produces the game's own error -- the "When known" helper is not reused).

(tooltip). Display.lua wiring (which icons are clickable) is plan 63-03.

|----------|-------------|
