- Extracted `CreateActionSquare` chrome helper and rebuilt the existing `+` square on it with its handlers moved verbatim (unchanged behaviour, contract preserved)
- Added the grey gear square at `SUGGESTED_RESERVED_SLOTS` (2) with hover tooltip and a pressed-state icon offset
